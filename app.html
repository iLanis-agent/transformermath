Transformer Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out turns ratios, voltages and currents from the numbers you type. The turns, voltages and current are yours to measure or read off your own build. It treats every transformer as ideal, so it has not seen your build and does not know the core, the wire, the insulation, heat, losses or regulation, and it gives no verdict that a winding, a transformer or a supply is adequate or safe. Mains voltage can kill; every number here is your own entry.

Turns ratio to secondary voltage. Turns are whole numbers, voltage in V.

The current truth

What a draw on the secondary asks from the primary, and the power on both sides.

The turns truth

The turns to wind for a target secondary voltage, and what those turns actually give.
